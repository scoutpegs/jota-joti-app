ScoutMap guide

ScoutMap is used by Scouting members for relevant event and registration tasks. Follow the current instructions from Scouts WA, check eligibility and event closing dates, and confirm any questions with the event organiser.

ScoutMap guide

A practical guide to finding Scout events, registering and helping your unit organise activities.

Make the adventure happen

ScoutMap is the official place to find many WA Scouting events, manage registrations and organise activities for your unit.

Open ScoutMap

Register for an event

01

Sign in safely

A parent or guardian may need to sign in using their ScoutMap profile and authenticator app.

02

Find the right event

Open Events and Calendar. Choose Branch for state events, or Formation for region, district, group or unit events.

03

Check the details

Read the eligibility, dates, location, cost and closing date before booking.

04

Register and keep a record

Use the reservation number to register. If paying later, check the Invoices section under More for the invoice.

Visit and contact

Boulder Scout Group meets at Boulder Scout Hall, 10 Hamilton Street South, Boulder WA 6432, Australia, and welcomes families from Boulder and Kalgoorlie-Boulder. Email gl.boulder@scoutswa.com.au before a first visit; families can try three weeks free. Age sections: Joey Scouts 5 to 8, Cub Scouts 8 to 11, Scouts 11 to 14, Venturer Scouts 15 to 18. The only regular meeting time published is Venturer Scouts on Wednesdays, 6 to 8 pm; confirm all other dates on the calendar or with the group.
