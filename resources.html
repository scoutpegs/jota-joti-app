Scouting resources, uniforms and badges

Use official Scouts Australia and Scouts WA resources for current uniform, badge-placement and programme guidance. Requirements can change, so check the official guidance or ask a section leader if you are unsure where a badge belongs.

Uniform and badges

Where do the badges go?

Scouts Australia groups youth uniform badges into three areas. Use the current official diagram before sewing a badge on, and ask your section leader if you are unsure about a badge or local variation.

Right sleeve — My Identity

Name tapes, district or region identification, Scouts4SDGs and other additional awards, plus section badges.

Left sleeve — My Journey

Outdoor Adventure Skills, Special Interest Areas and peak awards show the skills and achievements you are working towards.

Front panels and scarf — My Involvement

Milestones, special event badges, patrol or leadership identification and the Australian and World Scout emblems go in their designated positions.

Before sewing: check the official chart, position and pin the badge first, try the shirt on if possible, then sew neatly around the edge. Do not assume every patch belongs on the uniform; some activity and camp badges may belong on a camp blanket. Check the leader or current uniform standard before sewing.

Visit and contact

Boulder Scout Group meets at Boulder Scout Hall, 10 Hamilton Street South, Boulder WA 6432, Australia, and welcomes families from Boulder and Kalgoorlie-Boulder. Email gl.boulder@scoutswa.com.au before a first visit; families can try three weeks free. Age sections: Joey Scouts 5 to 8, Cub Scouts 8 to 11, Scouts 11 to 14, Venturer Scouts 15 to 18. The only regular meeting time published is Venturer Scouts on Wednesdays, 6 to 8 pm; confirm all other dates on the calendar or with the group.
