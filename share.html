Share a photo or story

The public submission service is not available until the group connects its approved secure service. Do not upload or send personal information or photographs of young people unless the group’s consent process has been followed.

For an enquiry, contact gl.boulder@scoutswa.com.au.

Share a photo or story

Help keep Boulder Scout Group’s history and activities alive. Every submission is reviewed before anything is published.

Before you submit

  • Do not upload photos or personal details unless you have permission.
  • Do not identify children publicly without appropriate parent/guardian consent.
  • Submissions are private and pending until an authorised admin approves them.
  • Submitter contact details are never displayed on public pages or in RSS feeds.

For a general enquiry, email gl.boulder@scoutswa.com.au.

Visit and contact

Boulder Scout Group meets at Boulder Scout Hall, 10 Hamilton Street South, Boulder WA 6432, Australia, and welcomes families from Boulder and Kalgoorlie-Boulder. Email gl.boulder@scoutswa.com.au before a first visit; families can try three weeks free. Age sections: Joey Scouts 5 to 8, Cub Scouts 8 to 11, Scouts 11 to 14, Venturer Scouts 15 to 18. The only regular meeting time published is Venturer Scouts on Wednesdays, 6 to 8 pm; confirm all other dates on the calendar or with the group.
