Scouting training and courses

Training requirements and courses are administered through the official Scouting systems. Check Scouts WA and Scouts Australia for current dates, eligibility and registration instructions rather than relying on an old saved event.

Training

Venturers can build real skills and nationally recognised qualifications, for free. Learn by doing, online at your own pace, or at courses with other Venturers.

Scouts OnDemand

Short online modules at your own pace. Log in with your member number from ScoutMap (More, then My Family Details). The Adventurous Activities streams help with Safe and Trained Participant courses and are good preparation for a risk assessment before a hike or camp.

Open Scouts OnDemand

SAIT certificates

The Scouts Australia Institute of Training offers Certificates II and III that build confidence and leadership, can count towards your WACE and strengthen your resume.

About SAIT

Adventurous Activities

Safe and Trained Participant courses run from time to time. Check ScoutMap for dates, or contact an AA team through the Scouts WA directory. Venturers can join AA teams too.

Personal Development

Courses don’t have to be run by Scouts. Certificates from school, Cadets, SES, Surf Life Saving and other community groups can count towards your King’s Scout Award. Take them to your Unit Council and check the requirements on Terrain.

Open Terrain

Unit Management

Courses run by Venturers, great for new Unit Councils and linking Scouts. Dates are on ScoutMap, and you can request a course for your group or district.

Scout Character Development

Course 3 is for Scouts and Venturers aged 14–16 and Course 4 is for Venturers and Rovers aged 16–20. A Unit, Group, District or Region can run one, and participants get free badges.

RST and Power Boat Operators

The skipper’s ticket course is open to all Scout members aged 14+, not just Sea Scouts, and usually runs over two weekends. Check ScoutMap for dates.

Turning 18?

There are required steps before your 18th birthday. Find the 18th Birthday Checklist on the Scouts WA general resources page.

Visit and contact

Boulder Scout Group meets at Boulder Scout Hall, 10 Hamilton Street South, Boulder WA 6432, Australia, and welcomes families from Boulder and Kalgoorlie-Boulder. Email gl.boulder@scoutswa.com.au before a first visit; families can try three weeks free. Age sections: Joey Scouts 5 to 8, Cub Scouts 8 to 11, Scouts 11 to 14, Venturer Scouts 15 to 18. The only regular meeting time published is Venturer Scouts on Wednesdays, 6 to 8 pm; confirm all other dates on the calendar or with the group.
