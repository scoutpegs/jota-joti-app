Parent Guide

Getting ready for JOTA-JOTI

This page walks you through everything to sort out before the weekend: setting up your Scout's account, installing the app properly, allowing the event's websites, and turning off screen time limits so nothing gets in the way.

JOTA-JOTI 2026: 16 to 18 October

Expressions of interest close Friday 9 October 2026, one week before JOTA-JOTI. This isn't a soft deadline. If the form hasn't been submitted and your Scout's account set up by then, they won't be able to take part this year.
Nothing to buy, no device required Everything for JOTA-JOTI is free. If your Scout doesn't have a suitable phone, tablet, or computer at home, the group will have a device ready for them to use on the day, just let a leader know ahead of time.

The easiest way through this guide
  1. Submit the Expression of Interest for each Scout.
  2. Wait for the account-details email. It is sent later after the form is processed.
  3. Install the portal on the Scout's device if the device requires it.
  4. Allow the portal and approved activity sites through the device's family or content controls.
  5. Test the 4-digit PIN before the weekend, then use the checklist to finish the easy jobs.

1

Submit the form and set up the account

This part is the same on every device. Do it first, then move down the page in order.

Fill in the Expression of Interest

This is an Expression of Interest. It starts the setup process for your Scout, but it is not the final event registration or confirmation. Do this first because it has a deadline.

Open the sign-up form
If you have more than one child taking part, submit a separate form for each one. Every Scout gets their own account and their own PIN.

After you submit the form, watch for your email

You will receive an email later with your Scout's account details. The account email is prepared after the Expression of Interest is submitted. It may not arrive immediately, so there is no need to submit the form again just because you have not seen the email straight away. Check your inbox and spam/junk folder.

The email will contain the information you need for the portal, including:

FieldWhat it's for
PINThe 4-digit code your Scout types in to log into the dashboard. This is the only one they'll need day to day.
UsernamePart of the account record, not something you need to type in on this site.
PasswordAlso part of the account record. Keep it with the rest of the details in case it's needed later.
Participant IDUsed by leaders to look your Scout up if anything needs fixing or resending.
Keep the whole email somewhere you won't lose it. A photo of it, or a note stuck somewhere visible at home, works well.

Log in

Open the JOTA-JOTI site and enter the 4-digit PIN on the number pad. On a shared family device, there's a Continue as Guest option too, which lets you look around without using an account, and a Not you? Forget saved PIN link if someone else's login has been remembered on the device and needs clearing first.

Why the site might look like a countdown timer

After you log in, you'll see a countdown, not the full dashboard. That's normal. It switches to the full dashboard by itself in the last 24 hours before JOTA-JOTI starts. You don't need to do anything for that to happen.

There's also a box on that same countdown page called Activity Suggestions. Tapping it opens a short form where your Scout can suggest something for the group to try during the weekend.

  1. Screen 1 is what appears the moment you log in, any time before the final day. It's just a timer, this is normal and not a fault.
  2. Screen 2 is the PIN pad. Type the 4-digit PIN from the account email, either by tapping the number buttons or typing it on a real keyboard. It logs in automatically the moment the fourth digit is entered, there's no separate submit button. The device can also remember the PIN afterwards.

Install it properly

How this works is different depending on the device, and on phones and tablets it isn't optional.

3

Allow the JOTA-JOTI website

Do this after installation. Allow the dashboard first, then the activity sites your Scout will actually use. You do not need to remove all family safety controls.

4

Adjust screen time for the weekend

JOTA-JOTI runs across the weekend, so a daily limit can get in the way partway through. Adjust the weekend schedule or add extra time while keeping your normal content filters in place. You can put everything back on Monday.

Set yourself a reminder for Monday to restore the normal time limits, so things return to normal for school nights.

Activities so far

This is only a preview, not the finished list. More activities are still being added and this page will change closer to the event, so don't take it as final. If your Scout has something they'd like the group to try, there's a suggestion form on the countdown page mentioned in step one.

You do not need to buy anything just to use this guide.Most activities are browser-based. Games such as Minecraft or Terraria are optional and should only be used if your Scout already has them or a leader has confirmed that activity. There is still plenty to do without buying a game.
