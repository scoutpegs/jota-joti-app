Scouts in Boulder, Western Australia

Boulder Scout Group is a local youth Scouting group based at Boulder Scout Hall, 10 Hamilton Street South, Boulder WA 6432. We welcome enquiries from families in Boulder and Kalgoorlie-Boulder and list four sections: Joey Scouts (5–8), Cub Scouts (8–11), Scouts (11–14) and Venturer Scouts (15–18). Young people build outdoor skills, confidence and friendships through camping, hiking, practical challenges, teamwork and community projects.

New families can try three weeks free after contacting the group. We will help you find the right section and confirm the next meeting before you visit. No uniform or previous Scouting experience is needed for a first visit.

Our calendar lists confirmed Boulder Scout Group meetings and activities when they are published. For current Scouts WA events across the region, use the official event listing; a date or activity is only confirmed when it appears in the current source.

Contact: gl.boulder@scoutswa.com.au. Meeting dates and locations are taken from the group calendar; do not assume a regular night for a section unless it is published.

A Goldfields group with local roots

Boulder Scout Group is built around practical learning, teamwork and community service. Our hall on Hamilton Street is part of the town’s story, and local fundraising helped restore it for the next generation.

Across Joey Scouts, Cub Scouts, Scouts and Venturer Scouts, young people get the chance to try new things, learn from each other and take on more responsibility as they grow. Families in Boulder and Kalgoorlie-Boulder can contact us to find the right section and confirm the next meeting.

Out and about

Camp, walk, learn navigation and build confidence through activities outside.

Everyone pitches in

Cook together, solve problems as a team and help with community projects.

Have a say

Visit and contact

Boulder Scout Group meets at Boulder Scout Hall, 10 Hamilton Street South, Boulder WA 6432, Australia, and welcomes families from Boulder and Kalgoorlie-Boulder. Email gl.boulder@scoutswa.com.au before a first visit; families can try three weeks free. Age sections: Joey Scouts 5 to 8, Cub Scouts 8 to 11, Scouts 11 to 14, Venturer Scouts 15 to 18. The only regular meeting time published is Venturer Scouts on Wednesdays, 6 to 8 pm; confirm all other dates on the calendar or with the group.
