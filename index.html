Add Jota-Joti as App

Please add this app to your home screen to unlock the portal seamlessly.

For iPhone / iPad (Safari):
1. Look at the bottom toolbar for the Share button (square with arrow).
2. Scroll down and select Add to Home Screen.
⬇️
For Android (Chrome):
1. Look at the top right corner for the Menu button (three vertical dots).
2. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
⬆️
