Cyber Safety Code of Conduct

Safety is our absolute priority. Please review these rules with your Scout before they participate in any online communication channels.

  • No Personal Details: Never share your full name, home address, school name, phone number, or social media handles with anyone on chat, even if they claim to be a Scout.
  • Use Only First Names: Always use your first name or your assigned Scout nickname when conversing on international channels.
  • Stay in Supervised Areas: Only use the official links provided in the dashboard. Do not follow links sent via private message.
  • Report Concerning Behavior: If a conversation makes you feel uncomfortable, or someone asks inappropriate questions, close the tab immediately and inform your Parent or Scout Leader.
  • Be Respectful: Remember the Scout Law. Be polite, friendly, and respectful to all international participants. Bullying or inappropriate language will result in an immediate global ban.

Parent Glossary

Scouting acronyms can be confusing. Here is a brief guide to the terms your Scout might use over the weekend.

JOTA stands for Jamboree on the Air. It is an annual event in which Scouts and Guides all over the world speak to each other by means of amateur radio contacts. Local ham radio operators volunteer their time and equipment to make this possible.

JOTI stands for Jamboree on the Internet. It runs parallel to JOTA and connects Scouts globally via text chat, voice chat, video conferencing, and multiplayer games hosted on secure, moderated servers.

ScoutLink is a global, non-profit network providing safe, supervised chat environments for Scouts and Guides. They host the primary IRC (Internet Relay Chat) servers used during the JOTI weekend.

QSO is an amateur radio term meaning a conversation or contact between two operators. During JOTA, Scouts will attempt to log as many QSOs as possible with other groups around the world.

3. Medical Details

Parents/guardians must disclose the youth member's known allergies, dietary requirements, current medication (type, dosage and frequency), and any other relevant ailments or disabilities on page 2 of the Y3 form, so Leaders can respond appropriately. Tetanus immunisation status (within the past 5 years) should also be recorded, along with Medicare, private health fund, ambulance cover and treating doctor details where applicable, in case a hospital requires them.

4. Water Activities Authority

This year's JOTA-JOTI weekend does not include swimming, boating, canoeing, kayaking, rafting or sailing for Cubs, Joeys, or Ventures & Scouts. Where a future Scouts WA activity does include water activities, parents/guardians will separately be asked to confirm consent and their child's swimming ability (50m unaided and 50m in shirt/shorts/shoes with a flotation device) before participation.

5. Photo & Video Consent

Any individual or group photo/video taken at a Scouts WA activity may be used for promotional purposes on a Scouts WA managed site. Scouts WA cannot guarantee that photos or video will not also appear on other, non-Scouts WA sites (for example, if shared further by third parties).

6. Adventurous Activities & Liability Waiver

No adventurous activities are listed for this event (marked "NA" on all three Y3 forms). By signing the Y3 form, the parent/guardian gives permission for the applicant to attend the Scouting activity described, including any listed adventurous activities, and authorises the Leader in charge to seek medical assistance for the applicant should the need arise. 7. Cyber Safety Code of Conduct

All Scouts participating in JOTA-JOTI's online chat, voice, video and gaming activities must follow the Cyber Safety Code of Conduct set out earlier on this page: no personal details are to be shared, first names or assigned nicknames only, official links only, concerning behaviour must be reported to a parent or Leader immediately, and all participants must be respectful in line with the Scout Law. Chat is moderated 24/7 by trained adult volunteers, with automated filters for profanity and personal information.

9. Other Platforms We May Use

Besides the JOTA-JOTI dashboard and ScoutLink (covered above), Leaders may use the following third-party platforms during the weekend depending on the activity. Each has its own separate terms and privacy policy, which govern that platform rather than Boulder Scout Group or Scouts WA:

  • Google (sign-up form & email): our registration form and email correspondence run on Google. Google Terms of Service · Google Privacy Policy.
  • Video calling (Zoom / Google Meet / Skype): some JOTA contacts with partner Scout Groups overseas are arranged as a scheduled video call rather than radio. Only Leader-supervised, Leader-arranged calls on official Group equipment are used — never a personal 1:1 call. Zoom Terms · Zoom Privacy.
  • Discord: some JOTI activities are coordinated with partner Units over Discord voice/text channels, always under direct Leader supervision on a Group-managed server — Scouts do not create personal accounts or join private/unofficial servers. Discord's own terms require users to be 13 or older. Discord Terms of Service · Discord Privacy Policy.
  • Minecraft (Mojang/Microsoft), optional: playing on the ScoutLink Minecraft server requires your own purchased copy and a Microsoft account. Minecraft End User Licence Agreement · Microsoft Services Agreement · Microsoft Privacy Statement.

As with every platform used for JOTA-JOTI: approved platforms only, no personal accounts or private chats, Leader supervision at all times, and the Cyber Safety Code of Conduct above still applies in full.
