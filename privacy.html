Privacy and photo permissions

This website provides public group information and contact links. Enquiries should only include information needed to answer the question. Photos of young people must follow the group’s permission and photo-consent process. A photo reference link is not permission to copy or republish that image.

For privacy or photo-consent questions, contact gl.boulder@scoutswa.com.au. Do not send sensitive personal information through public social comments.

Privacy and media permissions

What this website does with messages, calendar information and group photographs.

Last reviewed: 23 October 2026

We want families to be able to use this site without having to guess what happens to their information. This page describes how the website is currently set up. It should be reviewed if the site starts collecting information in a new way.

Contacting the group

The contact form opens your email app and prepares a message to the group. The message is sent through your email provider; it is not submitted to a website database. The email you send may be kept in the group’s mailbox so leaders can respond and follow up.

Please don’t email or post sensitive information through this website. For membership records, medical details, emergency contacts or formal permissions, use the official Scouts systems and the instructions from your leaders.

Photos and stories

Online photo and story submissions are currently switched off because the secure submission service is not connected. The form will not send files at the moment. Please don’t use it to submit photos or stories until the group announces that submissions are open again.

When photos are considered for publication, the group should follow Scouts WA and Scouts Australia requirements, check the permissions on record, and respect the young person’s wishes as well as the parent or guardian’s permission. A photo should not reveal a child’s full name, contact details, private circumstances or a location that could put them at risk. If you see a photo or story that should not be public, email gl.boulder@scoutswa.com.au and include the page link.

Helpful guidance: Child Safe Scouting — Scouts Australia and eSafety advice on sharing photos of children.

Calendar information

The calendar page reads selected event details from the configured Boulder Scout Group Google Calendars through Google Apps Script. The public calendar output should contain only details that are suitable for anyone to see. Private notes, attendee lists and personal contact details should not be placed in event fields used by the website.

State events are sourced from the Scouts WA published calendar. If the live feed is unavailable, the site may offer the older Google Calendar view as a backup. Dates, locations and registration details can change, so check the official event listing or ask the organiser before making plans.

Other services and links

The site links to external services, including Google Calendar, Google Fonts, Instagram and official Scouts websites. Those services have their own privacy practices. If you open one, the service may receive normal technical information from your browser. We don’t control the privacy settings or content of external sites.

The site’s hosting provider may also process standard technical request information to deliver and protect the website. The details can depend on the hosting and security settings in use.

Questions or corrections

For a privacy question, correction or photo-removal request, contact gl.boulder@scoutswa.com.au. We’ll review the request and explain what can be done. Removing something from this website may not remove copies that another person or an external service has already saved.

This page is a practical description of the website, not legal advice. Privacy obligations can depend on the organisation and the activities it carries out. The group should check this notice against Scouts WA requirements before enabling any new submission or tracking service.

Visit and contact

Boulder Scout Group meets at Boulder Scout Hall, 10 Hamilton Street South, Boulder WA 6432, Australia, and welcomes families from Boulder and Kalgoorlie-Boulder. Email gl.boulder@scoutswa.com.au before a first visit; families can try three weeks free. Age sections: Joey Scouts 5 to 8, Cub Scouts 8 to 11, Scouts 11 to 14, Venturer Scouts 15 to 18. The only regular meeting time published is Venturer Scouts on Wednesdays, 6 to 8 pm; confirm all other dates on the calendar or with the group.
