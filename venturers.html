Venturer Scouts in Boulder

Boulder Scout Group’s website lists Venturer Scouts for ages 15–18. The section highlights youth-led activities, planning, expeditions, community projects, outdoor challenges and practical skills.

The published regular meeting time is Wednesdays, 6–8 pm. Please email gl.boulder@scoutswa.com.au or message the group before attending to confirm the next session and location.

Hall: Boulder Scout Hall, 10 Hamilton Street South, Boulder WA 6432, Australia. Families can try three weeks free.

Venturer Scouts: Look Wide

Venturers run their own program: expeditions, service projects, travel and the King’s Scout Award. Youth lead, adults support.

Venturers across Western Australia

Our Venturers are part of a state-wide community with its own events, training and awards, run by Venturer Scouts WA.

The next step in Scouting is Rover Scouts, for ages 18–25: “Beyond the Horizon”.

Outdoors
Hiking, paddling, camping and expeditions.
Creative
Making, designing and performing.
Community
Service projects that make a difference.
Personal Growth
Challenges that build confidence.

The King’s Scout Award

The highest award in the Venturer section. Venturers complete the Venturer Award activities, then reach King’s Scout standard across leadership, outdoors, personal growth and community involvement.

Visit and contact

Boulder Scout Group meets at Boulder Scout Hall, 10 Hamilton Street South, Boulder WA 6432, Australia, and welcomes families from Boulder and Kalgoorlie-Boulder. Email gl.boulder@scoutswa.com.au before a first visit; families can try three weeks free. Age sections: Joey Scouts 5 to 8, Cub Scouts 8 to 11, Scouts 11 to 14, Venturer Scouts 15 to 18. The only regular meeting time published is Venturer Scouts on Wednesdays, 6 to 8 pm; confirm all other dates on the calendar or with the group.
