Visit and contact

Boulder Scout Group meets at Boulder Scout Hall, 10 Hamilton Street South, Boulder WA 6432, Australia, and welcomes families from Boulder and Kalgoorlie-Boulder. Email gl.boulder@scoutswa.com.au before a first visit; families can try three weeks free. Age sections: Joey Scouts 5 to 8, Cub Scouts 8 to 11, Scouts 11 to 14, Venturer Scouts 15 to 18. The only regular meeting time published is Venturer Scouts on Wednesdays, 6 to 8 pm; confirm all other dates on the calendar or with the group.
