Website terms

A few ground rules for using the Boulder Scout Group website and its event information.

Last reviewed: 23 October 2026

This site shares local information about Boulder Scout Group: our sections, activities, group history, news and events. We’ve written these terms in plain language so families know what to expect.

Using this website

Please use the site lawfully and respectfully. Don’t try to break into site administration, interfere with the calendar or submission services, upload harmful files, or post material that you don’t have the right to share. Don’t use the site to expose a young person’s private information.

Event dates and registrations

Calendar entries are provided to help people plan. Events can change, fill up or be cancelled, and an entry on this website does not guarantee a place. Before travelling or booking, check the event organiser’s latest details and any eligibility, permission, cost or closing-date requirements.

This website is not the registration system. Use ScoutMap or the official event page when registration is required. If information here conflicts with a current official notice, contact the organiser and follow the official notice.

Photos, stories and copyright

Only send material you created or have permission to share. Getting a copy of a photo does not automatically mean you have permission to publish it. Take particular care with images of young people and follow the group’s permission process.

Unless a page says otherwise, website text and group photographs are provided for the group’s community use and remain with their respective owners. Scouts, Scouts WA and Scouts Australia names, emblems and branded material belong to their respective rights holders. Don’t copy or reuse logos, photographs or stories without the relevant permission.

External websites

Links to Google Calendar, Scouts WA, ScoutMap and other services are provided for convenience. We don’t operate those services and can’t promise that their content, availability or privacy settings will stay the same. Check their own terms and instructions when you use them.

Accuracy and changes

We do our best to keep the site useful and accurate, but some information may be out of date while a calendar, page or external service is being updated. We may correct or remove website content when needed. Nothing in these terms removes rights or protections that cannot legally be excluded.

Questions

Contact Boulder Scout Group at gl.boulder@scoutswa.com.au if something on the site needs correcting, or if you have a question about content or permission.

These terms are a starting point for a local group website, not legal advice or a confirmation that every policy requirement has been met. The group should have the final wording checked by the appropriate Scouts WA contact before treating it as an approved policy.

Read the privacy and media permissions notice.

Visit and contact

Boulder Scout Group meets at Boulder Scout Hall, 10 Hamilton Street South, Boulder WA 6432, Australia, and welcomes families from Boulder and Kalgoorlie-Boulder. Email gl.boulder@scoutswa.com.au before a first visit; families can try three weeks free. Age sections: Joey Scouts 5 to 8, Cub Scouts 8 to 11, Scouts 11 to 14, Venturer Scouts 15 to 18. The only regular meeting time published is Venturer Scouts on Wednesdays, 6 to 8 pm; confirm all other dates on the calendar or with the group.
